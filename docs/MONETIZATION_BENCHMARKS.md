# Mobile Puzzle/Casual Benchmark Snapshot

**Research date:** 2026-07-26. **Use:** reference bands and model definitions only. These samples
have different populations, attribution methods, geographies, platforms, genre scopes, and time
windows. They are not a Teetertown forecast and must not be averaged together.

| Metric                    | Published observation                                                                                             | Scope / warning                                                                                 |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Retention                 | Puzzle D1 20%, D7 7%, D14 4%, D30 2%                                                                              | Adjust article published 2025, reporting 2024 puzzle cohorts; match-3 differs materially        |
| All-mobile retention      | 2025 median roughly D1 22%, D7 just under 4%, D30 0.69–0.79%; top quartile roughly D1 >30%, D7 6–7%, D30 1.6–1.8% | GameAnalytics 2026 report; cross-genre aggregate and report-chart readings, not puzzle-specific |
| Puzzle engagement         | Average session length 24.48 min in 2024                                                                          | Adjust puzzle cohort; definition may differ from GameAnalytics playtime/session measures        |
| Puzzle UA/monetization    | 2024 CPI $0.45; ARPMAU $0.54; IAP ARPMAU $0.31                                                                    | Adjust dataset; not ARPDAU, ARPU, ARPPU, or D90 RPI                                             |
| Casual acquisition        | Overall CPI $0.77; Android $0.14; iOS $1.41; puzzle $1.72 for Feb 2024–Feb 2025                                   | Liftoff/Singular report as summarized by Mistplay; channel/sample differs from Adjust           |
| Casual D30 ROAS           | iOS 47%, Android 15% in 2024                                                                                      | Liftoff/Singular casual aggregate; paid-UA ROAS, not organic revenue forecast                   |
| Monetization-model mix    | Casual apps: 47% IAP-only, 28% IAA-only, 21% hybrid                                                               | AppsFlyer dataset spanning Jan 2025–Mar 2026; describes app-model prevalence, not revenue split |

geography, store fees, and content/live-ops costs remain unverified. The low/base/high workbook

cohort and content-cost evidence exists.
