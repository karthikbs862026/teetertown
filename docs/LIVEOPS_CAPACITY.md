# Live-Operations Capacity

Live operations are a hypothesis, not a Phase-1 deliverable.

Potential cadence after evidence: one fixed-seed daily contract, one resident request, and a short
weekly themed chain using approved modules. No punitive streak loss, energy gate, or physics/config
variation in comparable challenges.

Before any schedule promise, demonstrate eight consecutive weeks of:

- authored/tuned/QA-approved content produced ahead of release;
- complete golden/failure/perturbation coverage;
- physical-device budget and lifecycle pass;
- support and rollback ownership;
- localization, analytics, economy, and creative packaging;
- no regression backlog carried into the next cycle.

Stop or reduce cadence when content cost exceeds model, QA slips one cycle, trust incidents remain
open, support load displaces core work, or ranking/content versions cannot remain compatible.
