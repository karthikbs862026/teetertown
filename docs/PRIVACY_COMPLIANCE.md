# Privacy and Compliance Boundary

Target hypothesis is general audience 13+, not child-directed. Changing age positioning requires
owner approval and a current qualified privacy/advertising/store review.

## Data minimization

Phase 1 stores local settings, replay fixtures, and bounded diagnostics only. Do not collect
contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, raw input traces, account identity, or personal
data. Explicit QA export is local and user-initiated.

Before analytics, ads, purchases, accounts, social sharing, or cloud save: create a dated data
inventory; define purpose, lawful/consent basis, age handling, access, security, vendor roles,
retention, deletion, export, regional/store disclosures, offline queue cap, and incident process.
Raw replay/input upload needs a separate policy and consent.

No document provides legal certainty. Qualified review is a release dependency when those features
or target markets are selected.
