# Marketability and Honest Creative Test Plan

The creative must communicate within five to eight seconds: “I tilt the whole tiny world; objects
move through trustworthy physics.” Use actual fixed-build gameplay only.

Candidate moments: bottle save, elegant one-motion delivery, visible platform chain, funny
classified failure, and recovery. A deterministic highlight detector may identify near-threshold
survival, leave-and-return, linked goals, low jerk/reversals, and absorbed impulse. Post-run camera
or still card cannot alter or fake the simulation.

Measure unaided mechanic comprehension, correct expectation of control, clip completion, share
start/complete, inbound challenge open, and whether acquired players enjoy the actual graybox.
Reject creatives that imply direct dragging, impossible camera/physics, production art not in the
build, or rewards/meta that do not exist.

Marketability testing starts only after the graybox passes core trust automation and has honest
visual capture. Retention alone cannot greenlight full production if the mechanic is not legible in
creative.
