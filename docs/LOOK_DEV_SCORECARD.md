## Direction briefs

- **Workshop Warmth:** chunky painted wood, linen awnings, ceramic props, exposed brass hinges,
  morning light, limited paper signage. Lowest shader risk and strongest contact clarity.
- **Paper Theatre:** layered paper/cloth facades over wooden mechanisms, graphic cut silhouettes,
  painted shadows, visible fold/hinge language. Distinctive and cheap if depth remains readable.
- **Brass Twilight:** darker stained wood, brass linkages, glass highlights, warm lantern pockets,
  restrained magical trace. Highest atmosphere and overdraw/contrast risk.

## Selection gate

Capture each at fixed phone/tablet/desktop views with collider/joint overlays and low/medium/high
tiers. Record asset hours, draw calls, triangles, decoded textures, material count, occlusion, and
rights evidence. A treatment fails if it hides contact, needs expensive effects for identity, or
cannot be reproduced by the small team.
