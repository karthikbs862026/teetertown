# Player Research Plan

## Questions

1. Does the first gesture communicate “move the whole world” without explanatory text?
2. Can players who state the strategy execute it within the intended corridor?
3. Do failures feel attributable to their action rather than input, camera, physics, or hidden rule?
4. Which input/camera variant minimizes overshoot and ambiguity on real phones?

## Participants

Separate novice casual players, puzzle players, experienced mobile players, left/right-handed users,
and representative target/minimum device tiers. Do not infer child suitability from family-friendly
art; target remains 13+.

## Protocol

- Fixed build/content/physics identity and randomized variant order.
- Observe first action without written control instruction.
- Use think-aloud for strategy, then silence during precision execution.
- After failure, ask what happened, what they would change, and whether the game did what expected.
- Review replay only after attribution is captured.
- Record comprehension time, first delivery, attempts, commands, reversals, overshoot, cancellation,
  latency, success corridor, failure category, comfort, and trust response.

Initial targets: ≥80% explain control, median first delivery <20 s, ≥70% complete level three
without hint in Phase 2, <10% random/engine failure attribution, and correctly understood tutorial
strategies execute within the automated/human corridor.

Automated tests cannot satisfy these targets. Consent, minimal data, pseudonymous study IDs, secure
retention/deletion, and no silent raw-input upload are required.
