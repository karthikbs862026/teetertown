# Economy and Monetization Principles

No economy or real SDK is implemented in Phase 0/1.

## Later economy hypothesis

One restoration resource and one soft currency only. Add premium currency only if a later product
model proves a distinct need. Natural session boundaries come from 30–90-second contracts and
restoration beats; energy is not assumed.

## Safe order of operations

1. Prove control/physics trust and content desire.
2. Measure retention and content capacity.
3. Model low/base/high economics with cohort-specific data and sensitivity.
4. Review privacy, consent, age, store, offline, callback, and receipt requirements.
5. Obtain owner approval before a real provider.

Potential later products: cosmetics, remove-ads after ads exist, and post-success reward multiplier.
Partial information hints and one rewind after a clear player-attributable failure are conditional.
Execution advantages and rescue after invalid/ambiguous failure are prohibited.

## Model structure

For each geography/platform/cohort:

`D90 RPI = ad revenue per install + IAP revenue per install - refunds/store fees`

Ad revenue inputs: retained active days, eligible sessions, offer rate, opt-in, fill, completion,
format/country eCPM, caps. IAP inputs: payer conversion, purchase timing, ARPPU, mix, refunds, tax,
store fee. Profitability also includes CPI, creative testing,
content/art/QA/backend/support/live-ops cost.

Low/base/high values remain **unset for Teetertown** until retention, session, offer, payer, country
mix, and content-cost evidence exists.
