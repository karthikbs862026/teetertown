# Art Bible — Foundation

## North star

> A handcrafted kinetic storybook town using tactile wood, brass, ceramic, glass, paper, cloth,
> painted signs, warm miniature lighting, whimsical asymmetry, and restrained magical accents.

This is an original synthesis, not a request to copy a living artist, studio, or existing game.
Phase 1 geometry is labeled graybox and is not a production-art claim.

## Visual hierarchy at phone size

1. Goal and required object.
2. Fragile risk and dangerous edge.
3. Active platform, hinge, joint, and contact route.
4. Non-critical architecture.
5. Decorative residents, cloth, signs, and ambient motion.

Gameplay silhouettes must remain distinct without glow. Use value, edge contrast, shape, material,
light, and motion. Danger and success require shape/pattern/motion cues in addition to color.

## Material language

- Wood: broad contact surfaces, visible grain direction, warm low-frequency texture.
- Brass: hinges, anchors, latches, and mechanical authority; bright but not emissive.
- Ceramic/glass: thin highlights, visible fragility, warning crack/wobble before failure.
- Felt/cloth: soft capture/damping affordance; the visible material must match the assist rule.
- Paper/painted signs: localization-safe frames; no critical text baked into runtime textures.
- Magical accent: restrained post-success heartlight only; never hides contact or risk.

Mass is communicated through silhouette, support deformation/stance, sound, and motion response.
Friction is communicated by material and surface treatment, not invisible values.

## Asset integrity

Meter scale; applied transforms; no negative/unreviewed non-uniform scale; explicit `VIS_`, `COL_`,
`ANCHOR_`, `GOAL_`, `SPAWN_`, `DECOR_` nodes. Visual surface, collider, pivot, anchor, and center of
mass must agree in overlay and stable rest. Primitive/compound colliders are preferred.

Every source records creator, source, license, modifications, approval, hashes, and runtime budget.

## Camera and light

The live camera prioritizes goal/risk/contact and stable drag meaning. Warm key + readable fill +
selective contact shadow; no excessive bloom, depth-of-field blur, translucent layers, or dramatic
shake. Low tier retains silhouettes, hinges, goals, fragility, and contact with cheaper shadows and
materials.

## Phase labels

- **Graybox:** dimensions, control, contact, and composition only.
- **Look-development:** one of three treatments on the same approved graybox.
- **Production candidate:** complete source/provenance, budgets, collider/audio integration.
- **Approved final:** physical-device visual/performance review, overlays, accessibility, and rights
  pass.

No code or filename alone can promote an asset between phases.
